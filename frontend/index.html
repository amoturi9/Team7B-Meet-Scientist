About Us:

Meet A Scientist is an online application designed for people to get connected with scholars and academics alike. Anyone, teacher, researcher, student, or even a scientist themselves can use this application to connect with a variety of scientists and researchers.

Getting Started:

To get started, create either a Basic or Scholor profile.

Basic Profile

Basic profiles are for ordinary people looking to connect with an academic

Scholor Profile

Scholor profiles are for scientists, academics, and researchers who are willing to share their knowledge with others
